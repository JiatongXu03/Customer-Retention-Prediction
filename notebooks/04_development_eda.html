Retail: recent activity and repeat purchasing

Definition sensitivity and transaction-code review

A pre-specified 60-day sensitivity is examined only at the first development origin. We do not choose the horizon that gives the best model score. Nonstandard stock codes are flagged for review, not automatically declared invalid.

Executed cell 7
r60=retail_features(valid,'2010-06-01',horizon=60)
assert set(r60.customer_id)==set(retail.customer_id)
sensitivity=pd.DataFrame([{'horizon_days':60,'eligible_customers':len(r60),'retained_share':r60.repeat_purchase_60d.mean()},
                          {'horizon_days':90,'eligible_customers':len(retail),'retained_share':retail.repeat_purchase_90d.mean()}])

What this establishes

EDA identifies hypotheses to investigate: relationship duration and contract structure in IBM, service friction in Iranian data, and engagement in retail. Different measurements do not yet establish a common causal chain. The next notebook checks whether a fixed baseline predicts held-out development outcomes.
